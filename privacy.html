PRIVACY POLICY

CRICKET 360 is an informational cricket website. This starter version does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information.

Information and services

Match information is retrieved from CricketData through a server-side function. Cricket headlines may be retrieved from Google News RSS and link to their original publishers. Those third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and analytics

This starter website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting providers may process basic technical logs for security and delivery.
